Example: 9, 5 (9 Yes, 5 No decisions)

Calculated Entropy H(S)
0.940 bits

- (9/14 * log2(9/14) + 5/14 * log2(5/14))

Entropy measures node impurity in Decision Trees. H(S) = 0 indicates pure single-class node; H(S) = 1.0 represents maximum uncertainty.

AI Course Syllabus & Documentation

Complete curriculum mapping for B.Tech CSE Artificial Intelligence (20CS31003).

UNIT I

Search & Intelligent Agents

Problem formulation, state space graph representation, uninformed search strategies (BFS, DFS, Uniform Cost Search), informed heuristic search (Greedy Best-First, A* Search), admissibility and consistency of heuristics.

UNIT II

Adversarial Search & CSP

Game theory, Minimax algorithm, Alpha-Beta pruning, Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSP), Backtracking search, Minimum Remaining Values (MRV) heuristic, constraint propagation.

UNIT III

Knowledge & Reasoning

Knowledge-based agents, Propositional logic, Modus Ponens, Forward and Backward chaining, resolution, First-Order Logic (FOL), unification.

UNIT IV & V

Uncertainty & Decision Learning

Quantifying uncertainty, Bayes' Rule, Bayesian belief networks, Decision Tree induction, Information Gain, Shannon Entropy.
